The report has these fields and nothing else is required:
- "schemaVersion": 2, "executionId", "activityId", and "kind" ("delivery_audit" or "test_audit").
- "outcome", the place the skill ended:
  "passed" when it found nothing left to build or test;
  "completion_phases" when it added phases to the plan for missing work;
  "continue_test" (test audit only) when it saved remaining test work for another session;
  "needs_you" when the work this report leaves cannot go on without an item for a person, when
  a critical finding would be caused or worsened by the step that outcome leads to, or when
  nothing is left to build and a blocker or business question not marked waived is open before
  the pull request. Judge it from the kinds the outstanding list below shows and the steps listed
  after this. Any other item lets the work go on: report the outcome the audit reached and list
  the item in needsYou, and Kiln reports it with the delivery.
- "summary": a few plain sentences on what the audit concluded.
- "completionPhases": the IDs of the phases this audit appended, [] when none.
- "needsYou": [{"kind","problem","action","waitsOn"?,"criterion"?}] for each item a person must
  see, [] when none. "kind" is one of:
  "critical_next_step": a critical finding that Kiln's next step would cause or worsen. Kiln
  stops at once, so the outcome is needs_you;
  "critical": any other critical finding. Kiln goes on and reports it first;
  "blocker": access, a resource, a permission or an action that only a person can provide and
  that work Kiln will still do in this run needs, delivery included. It holds the run;
  "business_question": a business question whose best answer is below 20%. It holds the run;
  "test": a test the audit cannot settle on its own: a criterion with no test, a failing test, a
  high-risk criterion still Weak, a row the audit marked stuck, or, once the test work ends, any
  row still missing, failing or Weak. Kiln goes on, and one still open at delivery is reported;
  "other": anything else the skill names for the person, such as behavior changed outside the
  requirements or a human action that no work Kiln will still do needs. Kiln goes on and reports it.
  A declared assumption, a request to confirm or review something, and anything the audit can
  settle itself are never items. A person action the requirements place after delivery goes in
  afterDeliveryActions, never in needsYou.
  "problem" is the problem and "action" the action the person takes. "waitsOn" says what waits on
  the item, required for critical_next_step, blocker and business_question. Add "criterion", the
  acceptance criterion's ID, when the item is that criterion's test row.
  To list an item again, give its id, problem and action. Leave out kind and waitsOn to keep the
  ones shown. Give a new value only when the item itself has changed, such as a blocker that no
  longer holds anything. An item shown with no kind must be given one.
- "settled": [{"id","how"}] for each item from the outstanding list below that is now settled,
  saying how; omit or [] when none. Account for every outstanding item exactly once, in needsYou
  with its id when it is still open or in settled; Kiln refuses a report that leaves one out.
- "changedImplementation" (test audit only): true when you changed anything other than tests
  and audit records, including configuration, dependency manifests or helpers that runtime code
  also uses, so Kiln runs a fresh delivery audit; otherwise false.
- "raised" (a session of test fixes reporting continue_test): the IDs of the criteria whose rows
  it raised.
- "afterDeliveryActions" (optional): person actions the requirements place after Kiln's delivery,
  such as a later production release; these alone do not make the outcome needs_you.
