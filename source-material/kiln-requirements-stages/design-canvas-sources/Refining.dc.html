What we're building

The few statements the whole PRD hangs on, as the draft has them. If one is wrong, saying so now is cheaper than after the refinement. The early stakeholder review replaces this when it is ready.

  1. 1A customer can check a gift card's balance on the website with the card number and PIN, without signing in.
  2. 2The balance page shows the card's last five transactions and its expiry date.
  3. 3After five wrong PINs a card is locked for lookups for 30 minutes.
  4. 4A signed-in customer can save a card to their account and see its balance there.
Not included Topping up a card. Checking a balance in the store's point of sale. Transferring a balance between cards.
