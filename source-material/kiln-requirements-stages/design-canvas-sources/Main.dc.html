Bring your source material in

Kiln starts from source material committed in this repository. That is a brief in your own words, with mockups or a prototype for anything that has a screen, or an intake record made from transcripts and documents.

Getting it there is a conversation about what you have and what you meant, so it happens in your own agent session and not in Kiln.

Use the Enso Method. I have source material for something new to build in this repository. Help me bring it into source-material/ the way the method describes, commit it on develop, and stop there. Kiln will take it from the PRD on.
3When the session commits it, it appears under Ready to start. This page updates by itself.
How much is enough

Several paragraphs on what you want and why, and screens wherever there is something to look at. Everything Kiln writes afterwards is only as good as this.

Already know the folder? kiln run source-material/<name> opens it directly.
